# Community Builder - Community Strategy & Setup

## Overview

Builds communities that become your startup's unfair advantage. A strong community provides feedback, support, word-of-mouth, and a moat competitors can't copy.

## 2. Platform Selection

| Platform | Best For | Cost | Setup |
|----------|----------|------|-------|
| **Discord** | Dev tools, gaming, tech community | Free | Fast |
| **Slack** | B2B, professional communities | Free (limited) | Fast |
| **GitHub Discussions** | Open source projects | Free | Minimal |
| **Circle/Bettermode** | Premium communities, courses | $$ | Medium |
| **Reddit (subreddit)** | Public discovery, SEO | Free | Minimal |

## 3. Community Setup Template

### Discord Server Structure
```
📢 announcements        (read-only, updates)
👋 introductions        (new members say hi)
💬 general              (main chat)
❓ help-and-support      (product questions)
💡 feature-requests      (product ideas)
🐛 bug-reports           (issue reporting)
🏗️ show-and-tell        (users share what they built)
📚 resources            (guides, tutorials)
🎉 wins                 (celebrate user achievements)
──── Team Only ────
🔒 team-internal        (private team chat)
🔒 feedback-review      (review user feedback)
```

### Community Rules
```markdown
## Community Guidelines

1. **Be respectful** — Treat everyone with kindness
2. **Stay on topic** — Use the right channels
3. **No spam** — Self-promotion in designated threads only
4. **Search first** — Check if your question was already answered
5. **Share knowledge** — Help others when you can
6. **Report issues** — Flag violations to moderators
```

## 4. Growth Phases

| Phase | Size | Focus | Actions |
|-------|------|-------|---------|
| **Seed** | 0-100 | Quality over quantity | Personal invites, 1:1 conversations |
| **Growth** | 100-1K | Engagement habits | Regular content, AMAs, challenges |
| **Scale** | 1K-10K | Self-sustaining | Community leaders, user-generated content |
| **Mature** | 10K+ | Governance | Moderation team, sub-communities |

### Seed Phase Playbook (First 100 Members)
```markdown
1. Invite your first 10 users personally
2. Post a welcome message with YOUR story
3. Ask questions daily (seed conversations)
4. Respond to every message within 4 hours
5. Highlight early members publicly
6. Share product updates first in community
7. Host a weekly AMA or office hours
```

## 5. Engagement Mechanics

| Mechanism | Description | Effort |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Welcome flow** | Auto-welcome + intro prompt | Low |
| **Weekly thread** | "What are you working on?" | Low |
| **Office hours** | Live Q&A with founders | Medium |
| **Challenges** | Build/create something with product | Medium |
| **Spotlight** | Feature community members | Low |
| **Early access** | Beta features for community | Medium |
| **Community roles** | Power users become moderators | Low |
